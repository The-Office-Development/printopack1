# Printopack website: how to manage your content

This guide is for the Printopack staff who look after the website. It assumes no technical
knowledge. Nothing described here requires a developer, and nothing here can break the site
in a way that cannot be undone.

Keep this document. It is the only manual you need.

---

## 1. The one idea to understand first

Your website has two states, and they are deliberately kept apart:

| | What it means | Who sees it |
|---|---|---|
| **Saved** | You changed something and pressed Save. It is stored safely. | Only you, in the dashboard |
| **Published** | You pressed "Publish to live site". | Everyone, on printopack.com.sa |

**Saving does not put anything on the public website.** You can spend a whole morning writing,
correcting, uploading pictures and rearranging pages, and the public site will not change by a
single word until you press Publish.

This is on purpose. It means you can work on a half-finished announcement without a customer
seeing it, and it means one deliberate button press is what goes live.

When you press Publish, the site rebuilds itself. This takes about one to two minutes. You do
not have to wait on the page or do anything else.

## 2. Signing in

1. Go to your website address followed by `/admin` (for example `printopack.com.sa/admin`).
2. Sign in with the email address that has been authorised for you.

Only email addresses that have been added to the authorised list can reach the dashboard. If a
colleague needs access, or someone leaves the company and should lose it, that is a small change
made once for you. Ask the contact in section 10.

The "Customer login" button on the public website is a different thing entirely. That is your
existing customer portal, and it is unchanged.

## 3. What is in the dashboard

The menu on the side is grouped the way the website is:

**Content** (the things that change most often)
- **News & Events** - announcements, exhibitions, fairs
- **Products** - the product catalogue, and the groups products are sorted into
- **Our Team** - managers, their photographs and roles
- **Careers** - open positions and their requirements
- **Success Partners** - client names, logos and countries

**Company**
- **Factory Departments** - a photograph and description for each department and warehouse
- **Quality System** - certificates, quality assurance, laboratory and testing
- **Social Responsibility** - environment, local community and international items
- **Our Values** - the values shown on the Company page
- **Gallery** - photographs, videos and advertisements

**Site**
- **About & Home** - the headline on the home page, the company story, the vision and mission,
  and the counter numbers
- **Offices & Contact** - every office, its manager, phone number and email address
- **Settings** - the company details shown in the footer and at the top of every page

The **Dashboard** screen shows a summary and how much picture storage you have used.

## 4. Changing something

The pattern is the same everywhere in the dashboard:

1. Choose the section from the side menu.
2. Click the item you want to change, or press the **New** button at the top right (it names
   what it creates, for example "New post" or "New office").
3. Edit the fields. A panel slides in from the side.
4. Press **Save**.
5. When you are happy with everything, press **Publish to live site**.

To remove something, open it and press Delete. To hide a news post or a job without deleting it,
set its **Status** to `draft` instead. Drafts stay in the dashboard and never appear on the
public website.

### English and Arabic

Most fields appear twice: once for English and once for Arabic. The Arabic field is marked and
types from right to left.

If you leave an Arabic field empty, that item will show the English text to Arabic visitors.
Filling both is always better. Please read the Arabic once before publishing, particularly on
anything that was drafted quickly.

### The order things appear in

Every row in a list has a **Move up** and a **Move down** arrow on its right. That order is
exactly the order visitors see: the person at the top of Our Team is the first person on the
team page, the first photograph in the Gallery is the one shown first. A line under the table
reminds you: *"The order here is the order on the website."*

Two things worth knowing:
- The arrows disappear while you are searching. Moving a row past its neighbour in a filtered
  list would not do what it looks like it does, so the option is hidden until you clear the
  search box.
- Reordering counts as a change, so it needs a Publish like anything else.

### The counter numbers

The numbers shown across the site (offices, countries, employees, customers, average experience)
are all edited in one place: **About & Home**, under Counters. Change a number there and every
page that shows it follows automatically.

The years in the market are not typed in. You enter the **year founded** (1997) and the site
counts the years itself, so it stays correct for ever without anyone touching it.

## 5. Pictures: the rules

Pictures are uploaded straight from your computer or phone in the picture fields. You do not
need to resize or prepare anything: the dashboard automatically shrinks and re-encodes every
picture before it is sent, so a 5 MB photograph from a phone arrives at roughly 65 KB. Quality on
screen is unaffected.

Each picture field tells you the size it is designed for (for example `1200 x 800px landscape`).
Matching it gives the best result, but a picture of a different shape will still work.

What is accepted: **JPG, PNG, GIF and WebP**.

What is not accepted, and why:
- **Video files.** Never upload a video. See section 6.
- **SVG files.** Refused for security reasons.

**Your storage allowance** is shown on the Dashboard screen, as a bar and a percentage. It is
500 MB, and because every picture is compressed on the way in, that is several thousand
photographs. Watching it is not something you need to do often. Glance at it once or twice a
year.

## 6. Videos: important

**No video file is ever stored on this website.** This is deliberate, and it is the single
biggest reason the site costs nothing to run and will keep costing nothing.

To add a video to the Gallery:

1. Upload the video to **YouTube** or **Vimeo** first (either is free).
2. In the dashboard, create a Gallery item and set its **Type** to `Video`.
3. In the **Image / thumbnail** field, upload a still picture only. This is the poster the
   visitor sees before pressing play.
4. In the **External video / campaign link** field, paste the YouTube or Vimeo link.
5. Save and Publish.

The video then plays on your website, inside your page, but it is delivered by YouTube or
Vimeo. Your site carries none of the cost and none of the storage.

If you create a video item and have not pasted the link yet, the tile politely says the link is
still to come rather than showing a broken player.

## 7. Publishing

The **Publish to live site** button sits in the dashboard and is visible from every screen.

Underneath it you will always see one of two states, followed by when you last published:
- *"Everything is live"*, and the button is greyed out because there is nothing to send, or
- *"Changes not live yet"*, and the button is active.

Press the button, confirm, and the public website rebuilds in about one to two minutes.

If you ever see a warning that the automatic rebuild is not set up, your changes are still saved
safely, but the public website will not update until that is connected. Report it (section 10).

## 8. What not to do

A short list. Nothing here is a trap, and none of it will destroy the site, but each one causes
avoidable confusion:

1. **Do not upload video files.** Use a YouTube or Vimeo link (section 6).
2. **Do not delete an item to hide it temporarily.** Set its Status to `draft` instead, where
   the section has a Status field.
3. **Do not paste text with strange formatting** from a design program. Plain text from an email
   or a document is safest.
4. **Do not assume Save means live.** Publish is what makes anything public.
5. **Do not share your admin sign-in.** If a colleague needs access, have their own address
   authorised instead. It costs nothing and it means you can see who changed what.

## 9. If something looks wrong

**The website does not show my change.**
You almost certainly saved without publishing. Open the dashboard and look under the Publish
button: if it reads "Changes not live yet", press it.

**I published, and it still has not changed.**
Wait two minutes and refresh the page with `Ctrl+Shift+R` (Windows) or `Cmd+Shift+R` (Mac). Your
browser may be showing you a stored copy.

**A picture will not upload.**
Check the file type. JPG, PNG, GIF and WebP are accepted; anything else, including video files
and SVG, is not.

**I deleted something by mistake.**
If you have not published since, the public website is still correct, so there is no emergency.
Contact the number in section 10 before publishing again.

**I cannot sign in.**
Confirm you are using the authorised email address. If it is still refused, contact section 10.
